⚠️ Important: Super Admin escalations are reserved for serious unresolved matters. Frivolous escalations may result in account restrictions. Super Admins typically respond within 72 hours.

Escalation Guidelines

✅ Only escalate if the admin response is unsatisfactory or delayed 48h+.

✅ Include all relevant evidence upfront to speed up resolution.

✅ Be specific — Super Admins review dozens of cases daily.

❌ Do not escalate the same dispute twice without new evidence.

❌ Personal grievances or ranking disputes are not grounds for escalation.
